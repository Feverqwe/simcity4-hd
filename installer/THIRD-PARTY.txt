SimCity 4 UHD Setup uses Qt 6 Widgets, dynamically linked, under LGPL v3.
To use compatible replacement libraries, run the setup executable with
`--extract-to DIRECTORY` (a new folder), replace the Qt files there, and run
setup with `--runtime DIRECTORY`. Qt 6.12.0 source,
including license notices, is available at:
https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.12/6.12.0/submodules/
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html

The installed mod uses SCD3D11 (LGPL v2.1 or later). Its release, source,
license, and third-party notices are available at:
https://github.com/caspervg/scd3d11/releases/tag/v0.5.0

The mod uses SC4GraphicsOptions (LGPL v2.1) for resolution and graphics
settings. Its release and source are available at:
https://github.com/0xC0000054/sc4-graphics-options/releases/tag/v1.4.1

Setup downloads Python, PyTorch, and the IllustrationJaNai checkpoint on
demand. These downloads come from the respective projects:
https://www.nuget.org/packages/python/3.12.10
https://pytorch.org/
https://github.com/the-database/MangaJaNai/releases/tag/3.0.0

The Russian game localization is based on the SimCity 4: Rush Hour Russian
translation v1.1 from SerGEAnt's Zone Of Games (http://zog.redsys.ru/).
The original translation author is uncredited. The v1.1 package credits
Razunter for resource extraction. SimCity 4 UHD incorporates proofreading
and corrections. The original translation is distributed free of charge
and its terms prohibit commercial use.

The Russian option uses modified open fonts. Their sources, copyright notices
and SIL Open Font License terms are in FONT-LICENSES.txt.
